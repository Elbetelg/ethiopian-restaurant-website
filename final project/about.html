A Taste of Ethiopia, Our Way.

Welcome to Habesha Resturant, where we are proud to share our deep-rooted passion for authentic Ethiopian cuisine. Inspired by cherished family recipes and a genuine love for the rich culinary traditions of Ethiopia, our journey began with a simple idea: to bring the flavors of home to our community. Since then, our passion has only grown stronger.

At Habesha, we are dedicated to using fresh, high-quality ingredients to craft dishes that are both flavorful and true to their cultural origins. While we honor traditional cooking methods, we also embrace a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere where everyone feels at home. Picture mouthwatering dishes, a cozy setting, and a team that’s enthusiastic about sharing the essence of Ethiopian hospitality with you.

As we continue to grow, we remain committed to excellence and are grateful for the support of our loyal guests. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a returning friend, we invite you to enjoy a memorable dining experience with us.

What We Offer:

  • Time-honored Ethiopian recipes, prepared with love and care.
  • Fresh, high-quality ingredients, sourced responsibly.
  • A warm and inviting atmosphere, perfect for any occasion.
  • Friendly service from our passionate team.
